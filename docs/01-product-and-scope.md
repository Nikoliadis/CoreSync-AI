# 01 · Product & Scope

## 1. Positioning

GymPulse merges four products that users currently run side by side:

- **Strong / Hevy** — the fastest possible in-gym set logger.
- **MyFitnessPal** — the food database and diary.
- **Fitbod** — algorithmic session generation based on recovery and history.
- **A coach** — the interpretation layer nobody automates well.

The wedge is the **interpretation layer**. Logging apps produce data and leave the user to
draw conclusions. GymPulse closes the loop: it reads your training, your food, your weight
trend and your photos, and tells you what to change this week and why.

**Product promise:** *"Log it once. GymPulse tells you what it means and what to do next."*

## 2. Personas

| Persona | Profile | Primary job-to-be-done | What loses them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alex — the consistent lifter** | 24, trains 4–5×/week, 2 years in | Log sets fast, never miss a progression, beat PRs | Any friction during a set. >3 taps to log = churn |
| **Maria — the recomp beginner** | 31, new to the gym, wants to lose fat and gain tone | Be told exactly what to eat and do | Overwhelm. Too many numbers, no guidance |
| **Dimitris — the data guy** | 38, tracks everything, exports to spreadsheets | Deep analytics, trends, correlations | Shallow charts, no export, no API |
| **Elena — the returner** | 44, was fit, restarting after a break | Sustainable plan, visible progress, motivation | Feeling behind. Aggressive targets that break her |
| **Coach Nikos — B2B (post-v1)** | Trains 30 clients | Assign plans, monitor adherence remotely | No multi-client view |

The MVP optimises for **Alex** (retention engine) and **Maria** (growth engine). Everything
else follows.

## 4. MVP definition

**MVP = one sentence:** *a lifter can log a full workout offline, log a full day of food, track
weight and photos, and ask an AI coach what to change.*

**In:** email/Google/Apple auth · profile & targets · exercise DB · routines · live logging with
rest timer & PR detection · history · food search & diary · custom foods · water · weight,
measurements, photos · core charts · AI chat with real context · settings · export/delete.

**Out of MVP:** social, challenges, leaderboards, barcode, recipes, meal plans, vision analysis,
wearables, admin UI beyond a read-only console, billing.

**MVP quality bar** — these are release gates, not aspirations:

- Log a set: ≤ 2 taps, ≤ 100 ms perceived latency, works in airplane mode.
- Food search p95 < 150 ms server-side.
- Cold start to logging screen < 2 s on a mid-range Android.
- Crash-free sessions > 99.5 %.
- Zero P0 security findings ([11](11-security.md)).
- AI never gives a medical claim or a sub-floor calorie target ([10](10-ai-architecture.md) §7).

## 5. Business model (shapes the architecture)

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | €0 | Unlimited workout logging, food diary, weight/measurements, basic charts, 5 AI messages/day |
| **Pro** | €7.99/mo · €59.99/yr | Unlimited AI coach, weekly/monthly reports, plan generation, photo analysis, advanced analytics, export |
| **Coach** (P3) | €24.99/mo | Multi-client dashboard, plan assignment, adherence monitoring |

Architectural consequences, decided now to avoid a later rewrite:

1. **Entitlements are a first-class concept.** A `subscriptions` table and an
   `entitlements` service gate features; no scattered `if user.is_pro`.
2. **AI cost is metered per user.** Every LLM call writes to `ai_usage_logs` with token counts
   and cost. Free-tier budget is enforced *before* the call, not discovered on the invoice.
3. **Billing runs through the stores.** iOS/Android in-app purchase is mandatory for mobile
   subscriptions; Stripe for web. Server-side receipt validation is the single source of truth.

## 6. Non-goals

Explicitly not building, so nobody designs for them:

- A medical or diagnostic device. GymPulse gives fitness information, not medical advice.
- A social network. Social features exist to retain lifters, not to compete with Instagram.
- A live video coaching platform.
- A hardware ecosystem.
- On-premise / self-hosted deployment.

## 7. Success metrics

| Metric | Target at 6 months | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 / D7 / D30 retention | 55 % / 35 % / 22 % | Above category median (~40/25/12) |
| Workouts logged per WAU per week | ≥ 3.2 | Core habit formed |
| Food-diary days per WAU per week | ≥ 4.0 | Diary is the harder habit |
| AI chat → action taken within 7 days | ≥ 30 % | Proves the interpretation layer works |
| Free → Pro conversion | 4–6 % | Category norm 2–5 % |
| API p95 latency | < 250 ms | |
| AI cost per Pro user per month | < €0.90 | Keeps gross margin > 85 % |

## 8. Risk register (product)

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Food database quality | Fatal — bad data kills diary apps | Curate a verified core set (~15k common foods) in-house; treat imported/UGC data as a lower trust tier with a visible "verified" badge and a moderation queue |
| AI hallucinating fitness advice | Reputational + legal | Tool-grounded answers, schema-validated outputs, hard safety floors, eval suite gating every prompt change ([10](10-ai-architecture.md) §7–8) |
| AI unit economics | Margin | Tiered models, aggressive caching, pre-computed context, per-user budgets |
| Logging UX not fast enough | Fatal for the lifter persona | Offline-first, optimistic UI, usability-tested in an actual gym before launch |
| App Store rejection (health claims, IAP) | Launch delay | Apple Sign-In from day one, IAP for mobile subs, no medical claims in copy or AI output |
| Scope | Schedule | The phased plan in [15](15-roadmap.md) is the defence. Phases ship independently |
